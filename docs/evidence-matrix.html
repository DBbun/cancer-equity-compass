Design decisions produced by the review

  1. Clinical eligibility precedes each care denominator.
  2. Language, ethnicity, cost barriers, and site remain separate constructs.
  3. Recommendations distinguish given, recalled, and completed.
  4. Acute outcomes, surveillance, late effects, and second malignancies retain separate time origins.
  5. Treatment exposures precede implementation of survivor-risk models.
  6. Fairness auditing separates discrimination, calibration, threshold behavior, sample size, and intersectional robustness.
  7. Published estimates may calibrate scenarios only when population and outcome definitions are reproduced with uncertainty.
