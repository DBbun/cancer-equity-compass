Technical documentation

Synthetic data specification

How deterministic randomness, conditional covariate relationships, outcomes, missingness, and known failure modes are generated—and how external fidelity, utility, and privacy will be evaluated.

Return to the Auditor
Current boundary. Parameters are transparent engineering assumptions for software testing. They are not estimates of CCDI populations, real disparities, or clinical risk.
Repeatable randomness

A deterministic seed reproduces the same scenario and cohort size.

Dependent variables

Conditional rules link disease, risk, treatment, care, outcomes, prediction, and documentation.

Known answer key

Each scenario injects a prespecified failure mode that the audit should recover.

Generation sequence

  1. Sample age, sex, race and ethnicity grouping, cancer grouping, and synthetic site.
  2. Assign disease-specific classification, risk, and treatment exposure conditional on cancer grouping.
  3. Generate survivorship setting, transition readiness, language, and cost barriers.
  4. Determine care eligibility, then care completion using clinical and scenario conditions.
  5. Generate adverse events and a two-year outcome from risk and care variables.
  6. Generate a noisy predicted probability and optional subgroup miscalibration.
  7. Apply documentation missingness last so missing is distinct from a documented negative outcome.

Examples of covariate dependence

  • Cancer grouping affects risk classification, disease markers, chemotherapy exposure, radiation, and transplantation.
  • Age and treatment exposure affect fertility-care eligibility.
  • Age, cost barriers, care setting, and scenario affect follow-up and care completion.
  • Transition readiness and cost barriers affect recommendation recall and completion.
  • Risk group, age, treatment, adherence, follow-up, adverse events, and scenario affect the latent two-year outcome.

The generator does not yet reproduce every real interaction, treatment-selection process, site effect, or longitudinal dependency.

Prediction origin, horizon, and loss to follow-up

The demonstration predicts a synthetic adverse outcome within 24 months. Its prediction origin is an implicit analysis baseline after diagnosis, disease classification, treatment exposure, and represented care-process variables are assigned. There is currently no calendar index_date, so this is neither a diagnosis-time nor treatment-start clinical model.

followup_complete is a care/documentation measure, not a censoring indicator. The current engineering time-to-event fields do not model informative loss to follow-up or use inverse-probability-of-censoring weights. A future model adapter must state its index event, look-back window, predictor availability, outcome, competing events, censoring rules, and horizon.

How quality is measured now

Current tests compare outputs with the programmed specification: seed reproducibility, schema and range validity, baseline and targeted missingness, pathway ordering, eligibility logic, identifier relationships, CSV round trips, bounded metrics, and recovery of injected access, site, survivorship, and calibration signals. This verifies implementation and scenario recovery; it does not establish clinical realism.

Planned external validation profile

DimensionCandidate measuresQuestion answered
Marginal fidelityPrevalence and quantile differences, standardized mean differences, Jensen-Shannon/total-variation distance, Wasserstein distanceDo individual variables resemble an authorized reference cohort?
Dependence fidelityPearson/Spearman correlation error, Cramer's V, mutual information, selected cross-tabulationsAre clinically important relationships preserved?
Analytical utilityReal-versus-synthetic classifier AUC, train-on-synthetic/test-on-real performance, calibration and discrimination deltasCan synthetic data support the intended analysis?
Temporal fidelityFollow-up/censoring distributions, Kaplan-Meier or cumulative-incidence distance, horizon calibrationAre longitudinal patterns preserved?
Privacy riskExact duplicates, nearest-neighbor distance, linkage stress tests, membership/attribute inferenceCould a release expose participant information?

No single measure will be called synthetic-data “accuracy.” Fidelity, analytical utility, fairness-signal recovery, and privacy risk will be reported separately.

Future longitudinal and causal work

Planned extensions include explicit index dates, longitudinal encounters, competing risks, administrative and informative censoring, and loss-to-follow-up mechanisms. Fairness audits remain descriptive unless a causal estimand and defensible assumptions are specified. Potential causal modules include directed acyclic graphs, prespecified confounders, propensity or weighting methods, doubly robust estimators, sensitivity analyses for unmeasured confounding, and transportability checks.
